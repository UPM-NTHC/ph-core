Example Bundle: transaction-example

Primary-care follow-up resource index

The resources below are inline members of this Bundle. Their FSH definitions are co-located with the Bundle and retain their PH Core InstanceOf profiles. Their IDs identify entries within the Bundle; they are not separately published example resources.

Inline resource Role in the scenario
Task/task-single-example A laboratory task for Juan Dela Cruz requested by Dr. Maria Clara Santos. The task is to collect a blood sample for a ordered fasting blood glucose test.
ServiceRequest/servicerequest-hba1c-example Completed HbA1c order for Juan Dela Cruz's diabetes follow-up on 15 March 2025.
Observation/observation-based-on-service-example Hemoglobin A1c lab result based on a service request for diabetes monitoring.
Observation/observation-bp-followup-example Blood pressure recorded during Juan Dela Cruz's outpatient follow-up on 15 March 2025.
MedicationRequest/medicationrequest-single-example Dr. Maria Clara Santos renews Twinact (Telmisartan + Amlodipine) for Juan Dela Cruz to treat hypertension.
MedicationDispense/medicationdispense-single-example Pharmacy dispenses Twinact (Telmisartan + Amlodipine) to Juan Dela Cruz based on Dr. Santos prescription.
MedicationStatement/medicationstatement-single-example Patient Juan Dela Cruz reports taking Twinact (Telmisartan + Amlodipine) for hypertension.
MedicationAdministration/medicationadministration-single-example Juan Dela Cruz takes Twinact (Telmisartan + Amlodipine) tablet as prescribed.

Shared core examples included by this Bundle, such as the facility and credential issuer, remain separately published so other scenarios can reuse them. See the scenario narrative and this Bundle's JSON view for the complete linked data.
