CodeSystem: PRC CodeSystem

Official URL: https://fhir.doh.gov.ph/phcore/CodeSystem/prc Version: 0.2.1
Draft as of 2026-10-17 Computable Name: PRCCodeSystem

Consolidated PH Core terminology for PRC CPDAS Council codes, CPDAS numerical profession codes, and PRC licensure examination codes. CPDAS council and profession concepts reflect the FAQ as of 12 January 2021; PRC examination concepts reflect the standardized list posted 18 March 2026. The hierarchy groups related concepts and does not assert an is-a relationship. The 2021 CPDAS table says its numeric profession codes are for CPD attendance sheets and is subject to regular updating. The 2026 Food Technology and Speech-Language Pathology examination boards have abstract local grouping nodes because the 2021 CPDAS table has no council codes for them; those nodes are not CouncilCode values.

This Code system is referenced in the definition of the following value sets:

  • PRC All Codes ValueSet
  • PRC Council Codes ValueSet
  • PRC Licensure Examinations ValueSet
  • PRC Profession Codes ValueSet
