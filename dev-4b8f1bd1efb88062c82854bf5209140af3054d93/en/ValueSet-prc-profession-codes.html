ValueSet: PRC Profession Codes ValueSet

Official URL: https://fhir.doh.gov.ph/phcore/ValueSet/prc-profession-codes Version: 0.2.1
Draft as of 2026-10-17 Computable Name: PRCProfessionCodesVS

The 66 numerical profession codes in the PRC CPDAS table as of 12 January 2021. PRC states that the codes are used on attendance sheets for accredited CPD programs, and notes that the FAQ is subject to regular updating. Use these codes for a qualification or practitioner role only when the CPDAS profession classification matches; the code does not assert current license validity.
