Worked case: emergency assessment

Miguel Reyes is a synthetic 45-year-old patient with a history of type 2 diabetes. On 12 June 2026 he presents to PGH with acute chest pain that began at 06:30. His ED encounter starts at 08:30 and ends at 10:30. This case demonstrates PH Core data relationships, not a clinical management protocol.

Miguel is distinct from Juan Dela Cruz in the primary-care examples. Their resource IDs and illustrative PhilHealth identifiers identify separate datasets.

Participants and setting

Resource Role in the story
Patient Miguel, born 15 March 1981, with an illustrative PhilHealth ID and coded Philippine address.
Practitioner and PractitionerRole Dr. Maria Clara Santos and her ED role at PGH. The physician's qualification refers to an illustrative PRC issuer.
Organization PGH as the service provider. Its identifiers are synthetic examples in provisional exchange namespaces.
Encounter The ED visit, linking Miguel, provider, participants and diagnoses.

The connected clinical record

  1. The ACS Condition records the encounter diagnosis, symptom onset and documentation time. The diabetes Condition records a pre-existing problem and is identified as a comorbidity, not the chief complaint.
  2. The blood pressure, heart rate, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation, temperature and pain observations identify Miguel and the ED encounter.
  3. The ECG and troponin record illustrative findings supporting the documented STEMI assessment. Additional glucose, HbA1c and lipid observations demonstrate related laboratory data. Their reference ranges are illustrative, not nationally mandated thresholds.
  4. The ED Composition links the patient, encounter, author, diagnoses and selected diagnostic observations. It is an Emergency Department Note, not a discharge summary. Its appearance in a transaction does not make that transaction a FHIR document Bundle.

What PH Core adds

  • Philippine patient/organization identifier representations and provisional namespace guidance.
  • Coded Philippine address components and PH Core participant/reference constraints.
  • Explicitly selected support obligations on the Encounter and other applicable profiles. The general Observation profile mainly localizes references; the rich Observation examples do not create extra mandatory fields.
  • A reusable record structure for a downstream emergency-care IG to constrain further when its local workflow and clinical requirements have been selected.

Transaction and individual resources

The ACS transaction packages these maintained example resources and their literal-reference dependencies. Each entry's request.url names the resource type being created. fullUrl values correlate references within the request and are not live clinical endpoints.

The transaction is an illustrative loading request. It does not promise a server supports create, transaction, search or reverse inclusion; inspect the target CapabilityStatement. Repeated POST loading can create duplicate records. A production implementation must choose its identity and loading strategy for its own contract.

For introductory HTTP exercises, use the informative FHIR REST tutorial. For the other stories and historical records, use the example scenarios.
